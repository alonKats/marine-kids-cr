## What we do — in one minute

Marine science education for kids in Costa Rica, delivered by a credentialed marine biologist who lives where the schools are. Not tourism, not a swim academy — curriculum-grade programs built around the world-class teaching lab on the Tamarindo coast: Las Baulas leatherback nesting, Playa Avellanas / Playa Negra tide pools, mangrove and estuary systems, seasonal humpback routes.

Schools contract directly. No resort gatekeeping, no field-trip vendors. The specialist a school doesn't have on staff.

## Program #1 — Marine Science Beach Day

**Format:** 4 hours on-site at a Pacific beach
**Group:** 1 grade level, 15–30 students, ages 8–14 (adjustable)
**Adults:** 2 teachers/chaperones from the school + founder + optional assistant

| Time | Block | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:20 | Welcome + safety briefing | Students name 3 beach safety rules |
| 0:20 – 0:45 | Marine ecosystem intro on the beach — zones from dune to subtidal, food web basics | Students name 3 zones + 1 species per zone |
| 0:45 – 1:45 | Tide pool / sand exploration — guided discovery, catch-and-release, sand analysis | Students identify 5+ local species, explain 1 adaptation |
| 1:45 – 2:15 | Snack + journaling — field notebook activity | Students produce 1 scientific drawing with labels |
| 2:15 – 3:00 | Marine threats mini-lab — microplastic sampling OR fishing-line count OR turtle-nesting impact | Students measure 1 local impact, propose 1 action |
| 3:00 – 3:45 | Synthesis circle — each student commits to 1 ocean-positive action for the month | Students articulate the connection between learning and action |
| 3:45 – 4:00 | Photo + wrap | Group photo, parent handout, teacher debrief |

**Materials kit included** (founder supplies): collection trays, magnifying loupes, field notebooks + pencils, water testing kit (pH/salinity/temp), microplastic sieves, bilingual CR marine fauna ID cards, first aid + sunscreen.

**School receives after each session:**
- Attendance list
- Photo / video documentation (parental consent required upfront)
- Curriculum-alignment sheet — maps to MEP Ciencias standards
- Bandera Azul points-contribution report (when applicable)
- Teacher debrief notes

## How the program scales by grade level

Same Ocean Literacy framework, four cognitive tiers. Pilot enters at one tier; the framework scales to the rest in Year 2.

- **K–2 (PYP early):** storytelling, touch tanks, habitat models. *The ocean is alive, and I've met it.*
- **3–5 (PYP late):** ecosystems, beach-debris counts, water-clarity logs, vocabulary build. *The ocean is a system, and I can describe it.*
- **6–8 (MYP):** ecosystem dynamics, water-quality testing, citizen-science participation. *The ocean has problems, and I can investigate them.*
- **9–12 (DP / CAS):** acidification, marine policy, research-question fieldwork — Las Baulas, Ostional, NOAA / PRETOMA dataset contributions. *The ocean has answers, and I can find them.*
