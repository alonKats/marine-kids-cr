04The framework

What is ocean literacy?

An internationally recognized framework — not my invention.
i.

Developed by NOAA and the National Marine Educators Association; in active use in 100+ countries.

ii.

Seven core principles, 45 supporting concepts, with a full K–12 scope and sequence.

iii.

Maps directly onto IB PYP Sharing the Planet and MEP Ciencias Naturales — biodiversidad marina.

MEP mandates that biodiversidad marina be taught — but most schools fulfill it with terrestrial content because they don't have a marine biologist. I fill that gap.

05Framework

The seven principles

01
Earth has one big ocean with many features.
02
The ocean and life in the ocean shape the features of Earth.
03
The ocean is a major influence on weather and climate.
04
The ocean made Earth habitable.
05
The ocean supports a great diversity of life and ecosystems.
06
The ocean and humans are inextricably interconnected.
07
The ocean is largely unexplored.

06Scope & sequence

How it scales by grade

K–2
PYP early
"The ocean is alive, and I've met it."

Storytelling, touch tanks, habitat models, tide-pool encounters.

3–5
PYP late
"The ocean is a system, and I can describe it."

Ecosystems, beach-debris counts, water-clarity logs, vocabulary.

6–8
MYP
"The ocean has problems, and I can investigate them."

Water-quality testing, citizen science, ecosystem dynamics.

9–12
DP / CAS
"The ocean has answers, and I can find them."

Research-question fieldwork, marine policy, NOAA / PRETOMA datasets.

09The ask

Two ways in

Option 1 · Recommended

Full year

  • Begins December, runs the academic year
  • 9 × 90-minute classroom lessons per grade band
  • Monthly visits with teacher co-planning
  • 3 field excursions (tide pools · turtles · mangroves)
  • 2–3 teacher PD sessions, full materials provided
  • Assessment artefacts for MSA & Cognia portfolios
Designed for renewability — teacher capacity outlasts the contract.
Option 2 · Lighter touch

Single project

  • One ocean literacy project per grade level
  • 1 field excursion
  • Project-based assessment
  • 2 teacher trainings
  • A clean way to test the fit before committing
