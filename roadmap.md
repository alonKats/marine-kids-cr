## Key risks & how we handle them

| Risk | What it looks like | Pre-empt |
|---|---|---|
| **Liability insurance gap** | School asks "who covers fieldwork?" and we don't have an answer | Alon resolves this Week 2. Non-negotiable before any contract. |
| **Budget cycle mismatch** | School likes program but their budget closed for the year | Offer Option 2 (single project, lighter touch) as a Q2 entry point — Jan/Feb start instead of Aug. |
| **"We need to discuss internally"** | Soft no, no follow-up | Set a specific follow-up date in the closing email — *"I'll check in May 18 if I haven't heard."* |
| **Journey wants to pilot just one grade band** | Could become a ceiling, not a floor | Agree to one grade as entry point, but write the contract so scope-up to school-wide in Year 2 is the default unless renegotiated. |
| **Effi gets pulled into too many simultaneous conversations** | Energy and prep quality drop | Hard rule: one school in active negotiation at a time. Others are *queued*, not *parallel*. |
